Loopcut for macOS

Installing
  1. Drag Loopcut into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it from Applications.

The first time, macOS says it "could not verify" Loopcut, or that it "may contain malware".
That is because Loopcut is not yet notarized with Apple, not because anything is wrong with
it. Get past it once, either way:

  Without Terminal
    Click Done. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to the line about
    Loopcut and click "Open Anyway". Then open Loopcut again.

  With Terminal
    Paste this line and press Return:

      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Loopcut.app

    It removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag from Loopcut.app and touches nothing
    else. Only run it on a Loopcut you downloaded from github.com/Samffprice/loopcut.

Afterwards
  Loopcut looks for new versions itself and installs one when you choose "Restart to update".
  Those updates never trigger the warning again.

Why not just sign it?
  Apple's notarization needs a paid developer account, which is on the list. Until then this
  is the honest workaround. Loopcut is a build of Blender with an AI agent inside; the source
  is at github.com/Samffprice/loopcut.
