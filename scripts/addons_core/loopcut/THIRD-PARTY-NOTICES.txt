Third-party material in the Loopcut add-on's media/ folder (the first-run setup)

icons/*.png
  Lucide icons 0.460.0 (lucide-static on npm, https://lucide.dev): search, sparkles,
  message-circle, loader-circle, circle-alert, check, user, key-round, cloud, laptop,
  circle-arrow-up, brain, minus, circle-help, box, person-standing, mic, audio-lines, aperture,
  list-checks, hammer, scan-eye and wrench, rasterised to 64 px and recoloured to Loopcut's
  palette. Licence below.

  Our own Cycles render of Poly Haven's CC0 model "Treasure Chest",

  listed so the source is known.
